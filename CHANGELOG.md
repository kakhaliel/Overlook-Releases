# What's new in Overlook

## v0.1.1 — 2026-11-25

New

- Overlook now updates itself. It checks for a new version at launch and every few hours, shows what's new, and installs it with one click.
- "Check for Updates…" in the menu-bar menu, and a switch for automatic checks in Settings → General.

This is the last version you need to download by hand: updates after it arrive on their own.

## v0.1.0 — 2026-11-24

First release.

- Now playing for Apple Music, Spotify and browser players, with controls and sound output.
- Tabs: Calendar, Timer, Mirror, Color Picker, Clipboard, Shelf (AirDrop / Copy / Cut) and System.
- Live moments on the closed notch: charging, camera and mic, headphones, Caps Lock, downloads, volume.
- Settings: your name, Pixel or Classic style, accent color, tabs and notifications.
